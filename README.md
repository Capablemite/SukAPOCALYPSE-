SukAPOCALYPSE
A Halloween horror mod for Digimon World 1 (PlayStation, USA version), by Capablemite.

The Sukamon have eaten File City. Etemon pulled you out just in time, and now his tree house is the last safe place on the island. Explore, flush the Sukamon generals, build up the tree house, and take on the King of all Poop at the top of Mt. Infinity.

What's different
A new opening where the Sukamon eat everyone, with Etemon's rescue
Etemon's tree house is your base: he sells items, saves your game, and reports on prosperity
His shop stock grows at prosperity 10, 20, 30 and 40
Prosperity 50 opens Mt. Infinity
Every NPC is a Sukamon, and recruits are PlatinumSukamon
Poop piles mark the Digimon who were eaten. Press Action on one to pay your respects
File City is empty, the Drill Tunnel and File Island Bridge are open from the start
The meat field gives 3 Sirloin a day
Birdramon joins you and flies you to all her places for free
King Sukamon is the final boss, with a new ending

You need
Your own original Digimon World (USA) `.bin` file, untouched. This mod doesn't include the game.
A PlayStation emulator, such as DuckStation.

How to install
Pick one of these. Each one leaves your original file unchanged.
Windows (easiest)
Unzip `SukAPOCALYPSE_0.5_Patcher.zip`.
Put `SukAPOCALYPSE_0.5_Patcher.exe` in the same folder as your original `.bin`.
Double-click it. It makes `sukapocalypse.bin` and `sukapocalypse.cue` next to your original.
Phone or any browser
Unzip `SukAPOCALYPSE_0.5_Android_Patcher.zip`.
Open the `.html` page in Chrome, Firefox or Samsung Internet.
Pick your original `.bin`, then save both files to the same folder. Nothing is uploaded.
Patch it yourself (any system)
Apply `sukapocalypse.bps` to your original `.bin` with Rom Patcher JS (https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/) or Floating IPS.
Name the result `sukapocalypse.bin` and put `sukapocalypse.cue` next to it.

How to play
Open your emulator.
Start `sukapocalypse.cue`.
Begin a new game. Saves from the normal game or other mods won't work properly.
